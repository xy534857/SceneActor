## 01-writings

1. **指数/复利模型（2019, “How to Be Successful”）**：把个人与创业看成复利系统；长期方向、网络效应和规模优先于短期优化。与 Lex #367 中“指数曲线”、算力/智能成本下降的表述一致。

3. **渐进部署（2023, “Planning for AGI and beyond”）**：反对“秘密造完再一次性投放”，主张在系统仍不完美时迭代发布，让制度和公众有时间适应。
4. **基础设施瓶颈（2024, “The Intelligence Age”）**：算力、能源、芯片和数据中心是 AI 进步的约束；资本投入和规模化建设是解除瓶颈的主要手段。

## 05-decisions

1. **Board ouster → return (Nov 17–22, 2023).** Background: nonprofit board cited loss of confidence without initially giving detailed reasons; Microsoft and employees pressured for continuity. Decision: Altman first accepted the board's authority, then organized a return effort; returned with a new board and Microsoft observer. Outcome: WilmerHale review; no employee/customer loss publicly reported; board structure materially changed. **Say-A/do-B:** said no one person should control OpenAI, yet used employee/Microsoft leverage to reverse a board decision.
2. **New governance board (Nov 2023–2024).** Background: old board was small and inexperienced. Decision: Bret Taylor, Larry Summers, Adam D'Angelo and later directors installed; board selection done rapidly “on the battlefield,” later formalized into slate/expertise criteria. Outcome: nonprofit board retained formal control until 2025 restructuring. **Value:** operational resilience and experienced governance over continuity with the old board.
3. **Nonprofit → capped-profit → PBC conversion (2019–2025).** Background: frontier training required enormous capital; original nonprofit/LP structure constrained fundraising. Decision: repeatedly “patched” structure, then completed recapitalization as OpenAI Group PBC with OpenAI Foundation controlling nonprofit. Outcome: October 2025 structure gave Foundation equity and control while Microsoft retained a large stake. **Say-A/do-B:** earlier language heavily emphasized nonprofit control and discouraged startups copying the model; later conversion made commercial PBC the operating vehicle.
